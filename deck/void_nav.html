03 · The concept

The Earth's crust is a map that cannot be jammed.

Rocks carry a unique, stable magnetic signature. A sensitive enough magnetometer reads it like a barcode; matching the reading to a survey map gives position, with no transmission.

06 · What is new

Four contributions that go beyond a lab magnetometer.

N-01

Vector, not scalar, map matching

The four NV axes give the full 3-D field. We match gradient direction as well as magnitude, which removes the ambiguity that limits scalar magnetic navigation over flat terrain.

N-02

Learned self-field compensation

A small on-device model learns the host vehicle's own magnetic signature (engines, current loops) during a short calibration run, then subtracts it in real time.

N-03

Confidence-aware fusion

The filter weights a quantum fix by local map "informativeness". Over magnetically bland regions it trusts the IMU; over rich anomalies it snaps the track back.

N-04

Spoof detector for free

Running beside a GPS receiver, VOID-NAV flags a spoofing attack the moment GNSS and the magnetic fix disagree beyond their joint error bound.
